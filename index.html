GUN ARENA: How to play and earn

1. The game
There are two teams: Cyan and Pink. Your team is random. The last team standing wins. Modes: 1v1, 2v2 and 4v4.
You have 100 HP, and every bullet that hits you takes 10 HP. When your HP reaches zero, a cross and a box mark the spot where you fell, and you can keep watching the match.
2. Controls
Phone: touch anywhere on the left half of the screen to move. Touch anywhere on the right half to aim and shoot: drag in the direction you want to shoot (the aim is exact), or simply hold to auto-aim at the nearest enemy. Tap ROLL to dodge; you cannot be hit while rolling.
PC: WASD to move, mouse to aim, hold the left button to shoot, Space or Shift to roll.
The camera follows your hero. An arrow at the edge of the screen points to an enemy who is off-screen, and the minimap in the top-left corner shows everyone on the map.
3. Practice (free)
No sign-in and no coins are needed. Fight bots on Easy, Normal or Hard to learn the game before you bet.
4. Online matches and bets
Sign in with Google and choose your bet per player: 5, 10, 20, 50 or 100 coins.
• Find match (ranked): you are matched with other players automatically and earn rank points.
• Create room (friends): creates a 4-digit room code that you can share with friends. No rank points are earned.
If the other team has a free slot, you can tap Switch team.
5. How to earn
• Win: you receive 2x your bet. For example, with a bet of 20 coins you win 40 coins (a profit of 20).
• Draw: your bet is returned.
• Loss: you lose your bet.
• In 2v2 and 4v4, every winner receives 2x their own bet.
• Ranked: +25 RP for a win and -15 RP for a loss. Ranks: Bronze, Silver (100 RP), Gold (300 RP), Platinum (600 RP) and Diamond (1000 RP).
• If you quit or disconnect during a match, you lose it.
Winnings are not guaranteed. Only bet what you can afford to lose. For players aged 18 and over.
