GUN ARENA: How to play and earn

1. The game
There are two teams: Cyan and Pink. Your team is random. The last team standing wins. Modes: 1v1, 2v2 and 4v4.
You have 100 HP, and every bullet that hits you takes 10 HP. When your HP reaches zero, a cross and a box mark the spot where you fell, and you can keep watching the match.
2. Controls
Phone: touch anywhere on the left half of the screen to move. Touch anywhere on the right half to aim and shoot: drag in the direction you want to shoot (the aim is exact), or simply hold to auto-aim at the nearest enemy. Tap ROLL to dodge; you cannot be hit while rolling.
PC: WASD to move, mouse to aim, hold the left button to shoot, Space or Shift to roll.
The camera is fixed and shows the whole arena, so you can always see every teammate and enemy and dodge in time. The see-through sticks never hide your hero.
3. Practice (free)
No sign-in and no coins are needed. Fight bots on Easy, Normal or Hard to learn the game before you bet.
4. Online matches and bets
Sign in with Google and choose your bet per player: 5, 10, 20, 50 or 100 coins. Everyone in a match needs the bet in coins.
• Find match (ranked): we search for players online and a countdown starts once two players are found. With only a few players you go straight into a 1v1 or 2v2, and with 8 players it becomes a 4v4. You earn rank points.
• Friends room: create a room with a 4-digit code and share it with your friends. The same bet applies and the winners double it. No rank points.
If the other team has a free slot, you can tap Switch team.
5. How to earn
• Win: you receive 2x your bet. For example, with a bet of 20 coins you win 40 coins (a profit of 20).
• Draw: your bet is returned.
• Loss: you lose your bet.
• In 2v2 and 4v4, every winner receives 2x their own bet.
• Ranked: +25 RP for a win and -15 RP for a loss. Ranks: Bronze, Silver (100 RP), Gold (300 RP), Platinum (600 RP) and Diamond (1000 RP).
• If you quit or disconnect during a match, you lose it.
🧱 Tetris Battle (1v1)
Pick Tetris Battle in the lobby. Same bets (5 to 100 coins), same payout: the winner gets 2x the bet, a draw returns the bet. A match lasts 5 minutes and the game gets faster every 30 seconds. Clear 2 or more lines to send garbage rows to your opponent (a Tetris sends 4). If you top out, you lose. When time runs out, the higher score wins. Use the taunt buttons to trash talk!
Phone: drag on the board to move, drag down to soft drop, tap to rotate, flick down to hard drop, or use the buttons. PC: arrows or WASD, Space to drop, C to hold.
♞ Chess (1v1)
Pick Chess in the lobby, choose the time per move (15 sec, 30 sec, 1 min or 2 min) and a bet. Winner gets 2x the bet; draw returns the bet. It is a 3D board with full rules (castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate). There is no total clock: if your time for a move runs out, your turn is passed to your opponent (3 timeouts in a row = forfeit). Tap a piece then a square, or drag it. Capture pieces to trash talk!
🎱 Billiards (8-ball 1v1)
Pick Billiards in the lobby. Same bets, winner gets 2x. Pot all balls of your group (solids or stripes), then the 8-ball. Phone: drag on the table to aim (or use the FINE AIM bar), pull the POWER bar down and release to shoot, tap the round pad to add spin. You have 30 seconds per shot; if time runs out the opponent gets ball-in-hand (3 timeouts in a row = forfeit).
Winnings are not guaranteed. Only bet what you can afford to lose. For players aged 18 and over.
