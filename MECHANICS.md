## 🎮 The game
- There are two teams: **Cyan** and **Pink**. Your team is **random**, and you can tap **Switch team** if the other side has a free slot.
- The **last team standing** wins. Modes: **1v1**, **2v2** and **4v4**.
- You have **100 HP**, and every bullet that hits you takes **10 HP**.
- When you go down, a **cross and a box** mark the spot where you fell, and you can keep watching the match.
- The camera is fixed and shows the whole arena, so you can always see everyone and dodge in time.

## 🕹️ Controls
**Phone**
- **Left half of the screen:** touch anywhere to move. The see-through stick appears under your thumb, so it never hides your hero.
- **Right half of the screen:** touch anywhere to aim and shoot. Drag in the direction you want to shoot (the aim is exact), or simply hold to auto-aim at the nearest enemy.
- **ROLL:** dodge. You cannot be hit while rolling.

**PC**
- **WASD** to move · **Mouse** to aim · **Hold click** to shoot · **Space / Shift** to roll

## 🧱 NEW: Tetris Battle (1v1)
Pick **Tetris Battle** in the lobby. Same coins, same bets, same payout as Gun Arena.
- **5-minute match.** The game gets faster every 30 seconds.
- **Clear lines to attack.** 2 lines send 1 garbage row, 3 lines send 2, a **Tetris (4 lines) sends 4**. Combos, back-to-back Tetrises and perfect clears send extra.
- **Top out = you lose.** If time runs out, the **higher score wins**. Same score = draw.
- **Win = 2x your bet. Draw = bet returned. Quit or disconnect = loss.**
- **Trash talk:** tap the taunt buttons ("GG EZ", "Sit down!") and your opponent sees and hears it.
- **Controls:** drag on the board to move (smooth), drag down to soft drop, tap to rotate, flick down to hard drop. Buttons for rotate, hold and drop. PC: arrows or WASD, Space to drop, C to hold.
- Practice vs bot is free (Easy, Normal, Hard). Ranked uses the same RP (+25 / -15). Friends room uses a 4-digit code.

## ♞ NEW: Chess (1v1, 3D board)
Pick **Chess** in the lobby. Same coins, same bets, same payout: **win = 2x**, draw = bet returned.
- **Time per move, not a total clock:** choose 15 sec, 30 sec, 1 min or 2 min. Each time it is your turn the timer starts. If it runs out, **your turn is passed to your opponent**. 3 timeouts in a row = you forfeit.
- A real **3D wooden board** with 3D pieces. Pieces slide and jump, captured pieces fly off the board, and a checkmated king falls over.
- **Trash talk:** capture a piece and a speech bubble pops up ("Delicious! Your knight is mine!"). You can also tap taunt buttons, and your opponent sees and hears them.
- Full chess rules: castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate, stalemate, draw by repetition. Resign or offer a draw anytime.
- Both player names and colors are always on screen. Tap a piece then a square, or drag the piece.
- Practice vs bot is free (Easy, Normal, Hard). Ranked uses the same RP (+25 / -15). Friends room uses a 4-digit code.

## 🎱 NEW: Billiards (8-ball 1v1, 3D)
Pick **Billiards** in the lobby. Same coins, same bets, same payout: **win = 2x**.
- Pot all balls of your group (solids or stripes), then the **8-ball** to win. Fouls give the opponent ball-in-hand.
- **30 seconds per shot.** If time runs out, the opponent gets ball-in-hand. 3 timeouts in a row = forfeit.
- **Phone controls:** drag on the table to aim, or use the **FINE AIM** bar for tiny adjustments. Pull the **POWER** bar down and release to shoot. Tap the round pad to add spin.
- The buttons are see-through so you can always see the balls. A bright guide shows where your cue ball hits, where the target ball goes (yellow) and where your cue ball deflects.
- Trash talk buttons (💬), practice vs bot (free), ranked and friends room, just like the other games.
