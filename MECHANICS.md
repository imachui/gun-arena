## 🎮 The game
- There are two teams: **Cyan** and **Pink**. Your team is **random**, and you can tap **Switch team** if the other side has a free slot.
- The **last team standing** wins. Modes: **1v1**, **2v2** and **4v4**.
- You have **100 HP**, and every bullet that hits you takes **10 HP**.
- When you go down, a **cross and a box** mark the spot where you fell, and you can keep watching the match.
- The camera is fixed and shows the whole arena, so you can always see everyone and dodge in time.

## 🕹️ Controls
**Phone**
- **Left half of the screen:** touch anywhere to move. The see-through stick appears under your thumb, so it never hides your hero.
- **Right half of the screen:** touch anywhere to aim and shoot. Drag in the direction you want to shoot (the aim is exact), or simply hold to auto-aim at the nearest enemy.
- **ROLL:** dodge. You cannot be hit while rolling.

**PC**
- **WASD** to move · **Mouse** to aim · **Hold click** to shoot · **Space / Shift** to roll

## 🪙 How to earn
Sign in with Google and choose your bet per player: **5, 10, 20, 50 or 100 coins**.

| Result | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Win** | You receive **2x your bet** (bet 20, win 40) |
| **Draw** | Your bet is returned |
| **Loss** | You lose your bet |
| **Quit or disconnect mid-match** | You lose the match |

- **Find match (ranked):** press one button and we search for players online. A countdown starts as soon as two players are found. With only a few players you go straight into a **1v1** or **2v2**, and with 8 players it becomes a **4v4**. A win gives **+25 RP** and a loss costs **-15 RP**.
  Ranks: Bronze, Silver (100), Gold (300), Platinum (600) and Diamond (1000).
- **Friends room:** create a private room with a 4-digit code and share it with your friends. Every player in the room puts in the same bet and the winners take double. No rank points are earned.
- Every player needs enough coins for the bet before joining a match or a room.
- In 2v2 and 4v4, every winner receives 2x their own bet.
